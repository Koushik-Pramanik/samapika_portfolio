About

Meet Samapika Chandra

Samapika Chandra is a dedicated dancer who believes that dance is more than movement — it is a form of expression, discipline and connection.

Through structured practice and personal guidance, she works with students to develop technique, rhythm, expression and confidence, both in the studio and online.
